Working with a travel advisor

What does it cost to work with The Motley Guide?

I don’t charge you a planning or service fee for a cruise booking. When you book, I’m paid directly by the travel partner. That compensation comes from the partner, not as an added fee to you.

Does booking through you cost more than booking direct?

My goal is to quote the same price you could get booking directly, or a better price when an eligible offer is available. Cruise fares and promotions can vary by sailing, cabin, timing, and availability, so I’ll review the current options and explain the price before you decide.

Taxes, port fees, and any optional extras will be shown as part of the quote where applicable.

How are you paid if your service is free to me?

Travel partners compensate me when I help arrange a booking. You pay the travel partner for your trip under that partner’s payment terms; my planning service doesn’t add a separate fee to your cruise fare.

Do I receive travel rewards when I book with you?

Yes. I offer travel rewards with every booking I handle. The reward and how it is delivered can vary by trip and travel partner. I’ll share the reward details with you before you book so you know what to expect.

Planning your cruise

What information should I have ready?

A few basics help me get started: your preferred destination or region, approximate travel dates, number of travelers, cabin preferences, and a comfortable budget range. If you’re flexible, that’s useful too.

Can you help me compare cruise lines and cabins?

Yes. I can help compare itineraries, ships, cabin categories, and available offers based on what matters most to you. I’ll make recommendations, then you choose what feels right.

Can you book Virgin Voyages?

Yes. You can explore and book Virgin Voyages through my First Mate link. If you’d like help choosing a voyage or cabin, use one of the Email Edward buttons on this site and share your preferences with me.

Who takes my payment, and when is it due?

As your travel advisor, I process your payments on your behalf with the respective travel partner. For your card information, I can send you a PCI-compliant invoice, or call you to collect it—whichever you’re more comfortable with.

With most cruise lines, the final payment is due at least 120 days before departure. The exact deadline depends on the cruise line, sailing, and when you book. If you book closer to departure, payment may be due at booking. I’ll confirm the payment schedule with you before the booking is completed.

What if I need to change or cancel my cruise?

Change and cancellation terms are set by the cruise line or travel partner and may depend on the sailing and timing. I can help you understand the applicable terms and assist with next steps, while any fees or refunds follow the partner’s policies.

Can you help with excursions, hotels, or travel insurance?

Yes. I can also help you look at pre- or post-cruise hotel stays, excursions, and travel protection options, depending on your itinerary and needs.

Hotels & all-inclusive resorts

Can you help me book a hotel or all-inclusive resort?

Yes. I can help you find hotel stays and all-inclusive resorts that fit your destination, dates, travel style, and budget. I can also help coordinate a hotel before or after a cruise.

What does “all-inclusive” include?

Inclusions vary by resort and room plan. Meals, drinks, activities, transfers, gratuities, and premium experiences may be handled differently from one property to another. I’ll help review what is included in the option you’re considering and point out any additional costs.

Are hotel and resort bookings also free to plan?

I don’t charge a separate planning fee for hotel or all-inclusive resort bookings. The travel partner pays me directly when I arrange a booking. I also offer travel rewards with every booking I handle, and I’ll explain the reward details before you book.

Who sets hotel payment and cancellation terms?

The hotel, resort, or travel partner sets the deposit, payment, change, and cancellation terms. These can differ by property, rate, and booking date. I’ll help you review the terms before you decide.

Flights

Can you book my flights?

I don’t book flights. If a delay, cancellation, or other travel interruption happens, booking airfare through an intermediary can add a step: the airline may direct ticket changes back through the original booking channel, which can make it harder to resolve things directly with customer service at the airport and create extra logistical challenges.

I recommend booking flights directly with the airline so you can work with its customer service team yourself if plans are disrupted.
